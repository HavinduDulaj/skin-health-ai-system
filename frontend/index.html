    Risk awareness

    How to read the grade.

    Low, Medium, and High are screening categories. They are not disease names. This dashboard is the guidance layer from the proposal.

    Low risk

    Minor or common appearance. Monitor with ordinary skincare. Photograph again in a few days. See a clinician if it spreads or becomes painful.

    Medium risk

    May need observation. If it persists or worsens, seek professional dermatological guidance. Medium is also the weakest visual boundary in this dataset.

    High risk

    Stronger abnormality indicators. Consult a dermatologist. This is not an emergency diagnosis and not a substitute for urgent care if symptoms escalate.

    When the model abstains

    If confidence is low or the top-two grades are close, no winner is forced. Retake the photograph or see a clinician rather than guessing.

      Method

      Built to be honest when the photographs are not separable.

      The screening head separates Low, Medium, and High for acne, burns, rash, and warts. Cleaning can remove duplicates, blur, and train/test leakage. It cannot invent a visual boundary that is not in the pictures.

      1. Validate files, find exact and near-duplicates, score quality.
      2. Flag inconsistent labels inside each lesion type. No auto-relabel.
      3. Rebuild a stratified 70 / 15 / 15 split. Near-duplicate groups stay together.
      4. Train EfficientNetV2-B0 with a lesion one-hot. Test is read once.
      5. Serve grades through a versioned API that may abstain, reject the frame, and show Grad-CAM.

      Proposal architecture (this component)

        This product is a screening aid for four lesion families — acne, burns, rash, and warts — and three risk grades. It is not a melanoma detector, not an emergency service, and not medical advice.
